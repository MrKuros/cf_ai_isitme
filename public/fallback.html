Is it down, or is it me?

Fallback page for when the main IsItMe app can't load. It runs only in your browser: no server, no Cloudflare edge checks.

Browser-only verdict

Browsers can't see status codes, so this can only tell "reachable" from "not reachable". The full app also checks from five Cloudflare regions, Radar and other users.
